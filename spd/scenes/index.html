Ecosystem direction
Commercial doctrine

Ownership and capability are separate

A future Free user may purchase an individual Scene without first subscribing to Pro. Purchased content and SPD capability tier are separate relationships.

Engineering rule

Scenes consume SPD telemetry

A Scene is not allowed to become its own sensor engine. It should present the same normalized truth used elsewhere in SPD.
