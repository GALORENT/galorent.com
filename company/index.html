Why GALORENT exists

The machine should not be a black box.

Most hardware tools stop at showing numbers. GALORENT is being built around the full chain from observation to evidence to controlled testing to interpretation.

01Measure honestlyNormalized machine observations before conclusions.
02Preserve evidenceWhat happened remains reviewable after the moment passes.
03Test deliberatelyControlled workloads create comparable machine evidence.
04Explain the machineThe product should translate hardware behavior into useful understanding.
